Services

Shaped by hand, one thread at a time.

About

More than three decades of beauty, from Pakistan to Pennsylvania.

  1. Pakistan

    Farah spends 22 years working in beauty salons, mastering threading and the traditional techniques behind everything we do.

  2. 2011

    She earns her Pennsylvania esthetician license and opens her own studio in Harrisburg, PA.

  3. 2018

    The studio moves to Lancaster and opens inside Park City Center.

  4. 2021

    Farrah's moves into its own space at 1360A Harrisburg Pike and grows into a full beauty studio.

  5. 2026

    We return to Park City Center with a second location, so your brows are never far away.

Farrah's is woman-owned and family-run. Farah learned her craft the traditional way, over 22 years in beauty salons in Pakistan, where threading is passed from one pair of hands to the next. She brought that training to Pennsylvania, earned her esthetician license in 2011, and has been shaping brows for Pennsylvania clients ever since: first in Harrisburg, and since 2018 in Lancaster.

Every technician on the team trains in the same technique and the same standards. We keep things simple: fresh thread and supplies for every client, straight advice about what will suit your face, and no pressure to add on anything you don't want. Our clients come back every few weeks, and they bring their mothers, daughters, and friends.

"A good brow doesn't announce itself. It just makes everything else look right."
