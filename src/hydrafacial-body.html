What it is

A facial that does the work of several.

A HydraFacial isn't a mask and a massage. A handheld device with a spiral tip moves across the skin and, in one pass, washes away dead skin and oil, delivers a gentle peel, pulls debris out of pores with painless suction, and pushes hydrating serums back in. Every step is adjustable, so first-time and sensitive skin get a softer setting and congested or oily skin gets a deeper one.

Good for

  • Clogged pores and blackheads
  • Breakouts and oily skin
  • Dull, tired or dehydrated skin
  • Fine lines and loss of firmness
  • Uneven texture and tone
  • A big event this week

HydraFacial reports the treatment is clinically shown to visibly improve mild-to-moderate acne. If you're on prescription acne medication or a retinoid, tell us when you book and we'll adjust the peel.

The treatment, step by step

What happens in the chair.

  1. 1

    Lymphatic drainage Platinum

    Gentle suction along the lymph pathways to reduce puffiness and give the face a lifted, sculpted look before the facial starts.

  2. 2

    Cleanse and microdermabrasion

    The spiral tip lifts away dead skin cells and surface oil so everything that follows reaches fresh skin.

  3. 3

    Customized peel

    A mild glycolic and salicylic blend loosens debris in the pores and brightens, chosen at a strength that suits your skin. No peeling afterward.

  4. 4

    Extraction

    Painless vortex suction clears blackheads and congestion from the pores. No squeezing, no redness.

  5. 5

    Targeted booster Deluxe · Platinum

    A concentrated serum for your concern: brightening, firming, clarifying, or extra hydration. Ask which boosters we carry.

  6. 6

    LED light therapy Deluxe · Platinum

    Red light to support collagen and calm the skin, or blue light to help with acne-causing bacteria. You choose.

  7. 7

    Hydration infusion

    An antioxidant and peptide serum is pressed into the skin for the dewy, plump finish HydraFacial is known for.

Does it hurt, and is there downtime?

No. Most people describe it as a cool, tingly sweep across the face. There is no downtime: you can put on makeup and go about your day right after. Skin may look slightly flushed for an hour or so.

How long does it take?

Plan on about 30 minutes for a Signature and closer to 45 to 60 minutes for Deluxe and Platinum. We'll confirm the time when we call to book you.

How often should I get one?

Results are visible after a single treatment and build with each visit. For lasting change, most clients come every four to six weeks.

Is it safe for sensitive skin?

Yes. Tip, suction and peel strength are all adjustable, and we start gently for first-time and sensitive skin. Tell us about any prescription skincare, recent procedures, or allergies when you book.

How should I prepare?

Come with a clean face if you can, and skip retinol, exfoliating acids and waxing on the face for a couple of days beforehand. Brow threading can be done the same visit; we'll do it first.

Yes. Gift cards are sold at both studios in any amount and can be used toward any tier. Reserve one for pickup.
